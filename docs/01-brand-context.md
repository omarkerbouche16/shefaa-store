# 01 - Brand Context

## Brand

Arabic name: `الشفاء`

English name: `shefaa`

Domain: `shefaa.shop`

Backend domain: `api.shefaa.shop`

Positioning: a premium Algerian desert wellness brand that brings traditional natural remedies into a trustworthy modern DTC experience. The store must feel like it owns, curates, tests, and stands behind the products, not like a generic dropshipping catalog.

Core promise:

> نقاوة الصحراء، حكمة الأجداد، وراحة الاستعمال اليومي.

Brand idea:

> الشفاء يجمع أعشاب، زيوت، عسل ومكملات طبيعية مستوحاة من طب الصحراء والتقاليد الجزائرية، مع عرض واضح، ثقة عالية، وخدمة دفع عند الاستلام.

## Target Audience

Primary ICP:

- Algerian adults, especially people in southern Algeria and desert regions.
- Age 25-55, Arabic-first, comfortable with Algerian dialect and simple classical Arabic.
- Buyers who trust traditional medicine, family recommendations, natural ingredients, and visible proof.
- COD shoppers who need reassurance before giving their phone number.
- TikTok, Snapchat, and Facebook users who arrive from UGC, AI video, before/after-style content, testimonials, and offer ads.

Key buyer segments:

- Women 25-45: hair loss, skin glow, wrinkles, collagen, shea butter, argan oil, natural beauty.
- Men and women 30-55: fatigue, memory, energy, stress, sleep, joint comfort, natural supplements.
- Family buyers: honey with nuts, sesame, arak, khuzama, desert herbs, daily wellness.
- Gift buyers: premium bundles for mothers, wives, and family.

## Buyer Psychology

What they want to feel:

- "هذا منتج طبيعي ونقي."
- "العلامة باينة موثوقة ومش صفحة عشوائية."
- "ناس من بلادي جربوه."
- "السعر مفهوم، والعرض يشجعني ندي 2 ولا 3."
- "الدفع عند الاستلام، ما نخسر والو قبل ما يوصلني."

Main anxieties:

- Fake products or repackaged cheap imports.
- Exaggerated medical claims.
- Delivery uncertainty and unwanted phone spam.
- Product quality, smell, taste, and side effects.
- Whether the product is appropriate for pregnant women, chronic disease, or medication users.

Trust levers:

- COD, order confirmation call, clear phone validation, delivery promise by wilaya.
- Ingredient transparency and usage instructions.
- "Inspired by traditional use" plus science-backed ingredient explanations.
- Real reviews with Algerian names/wilayas.
- Certifications/trust badges phrased carefully: natural origin, quality checked, hygienic packaging, supplier verification. Only show actual lab certificates once available.
- No fake medical guarantees. Use benefit language, not disease-cure promises.

## Voice

Use warm Algerian Arabic with premium restraint. Avoid cheap hype.

Preferred language mix:

- Headlines: Arabic, often emotional.
- Body: Arabic with light Algerian dialect.
- Technical proof: simple Arabic with ingredient names.
- Buttons: short and direct.

Examples:

- `اختار عرضك وخليه يوصلك حتى للدار`
- `طبيعي، نقي، ومستوحى من أسرار الصحراء`
- `كمية محدودة من دفعة اليوم`
- `الدفع عند الاستلام بعد تأكيد الطلب`
- `مكونات معروفة في الاستعمال التقليدي`

Avoid:

- "يعالج نهائيا", "مضمون 100%", "نتائج في يومين", disease-cure claims.
- Overloaded popups or aggressive countdowns that look scammy.
- Generic English wellness copy.

## Product Promise Boundaries

Allowed:

- Supports hair health, skin glow, energy, focus, relaxation, daily wellness.
- Traditionally used for comfort, vitality, beauty routines.
- Explain ingredients and common nutritional roles.

Not allowed unless legally verified:

- Cures hair loss, diabetes, infertility, cancer, autoimmune disease, depression, or chronic medical conditions.
- FDA/medical certification claims without proof.
- Fake before/after photos.

Add disclaimer in footer and product pages:

> منتجات الشفاء طبيعية ومكملات غذائية/عناية، وليست بديلا عن استشارة الطبيب. إذا كنت حاملا، مرضعا، تستعمل دواء، أو لديك مرض مزمن، استشر طبيبك قبل الاستعمال.
