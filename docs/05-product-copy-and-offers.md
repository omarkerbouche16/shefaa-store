# 05 - Product Copy and Offers

## Pricing Rules

All core products use:

- 1 piece: `1999 دج`
- 2 pieces: `2790 دج`
- 3 pieces: `3490 دج`

Offer card order:

1. 2 pieces, marked `الأكثر طلبا`.
2. 3 pieces, marked `أفضل قيمة`.
3. 1 piece, marked `للتجربة`.

Default selected offer:

- Beauty and supplements: 3 pieces.
- Food/herbs/oils: 2 pieces.

Post-submit upsell:

- One relevant product only.
- Price: `999 دج`.
- Duration: 10-15 seconds.
- This is the only discount experience.
